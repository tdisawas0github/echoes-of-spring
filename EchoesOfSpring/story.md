# Echoes of Spring — Complete Story & Branching Paths

> A romance / slice-of-life visual novel set in the small cherry-blossom town of **Hanamachi**.

---

## Characters

| Character | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **You** (player-named) | A college student moving to Hanamachi for your final year of studies. |
| **Sakura** | A warm, spirited barista and amateur painter at *Petal Brew*. Shoulder-length wavy pink-brown hair, flower clip, paint-stained apron. |
| **Akira** | A quiet, thoughtful bookshop owner at *Ink & Pages*. Short tousled dark hair, reading glasses, dark blue cardigan. His cat is named Hemingway. |

## Chapter 1 — A New Beginning

You arrive in Hanamachi by bus on a spring day, cherry-blossom petals swirling through the air. The town has a population of 3,200 — far smaller than you expected. Your grandmother mailed you a hand-drawn map. Two places catch your eye on the main street: a cozy café called **Petal Brew** and a narrow bookshop called **Ink & Pages**.

### Choice: Where do you go first?

---

### Path A — Step into Petal Brew (the café)

A bell rings as you enter. The café smells of freshly ground coffee. **Sakura** greets you — she's the barista, amateur painter, and self-described "professional overthinker." She offers you a lavender latte on the house.

**Choice:**
- *"That sounds wonderful, thank you!"* → Sakura +2. She likes that you're adventurous with coffee.
- *"I'll just take a regular drip, thanks."* → Sakura +1. She respects a purist.
- *"Actually, I should get going…"* → No points. You leave early.

If you stay, you notice watercolor paintings on every wall — sunsets, flowers, the town square. You ask if she painted them. She says painting keeps her sane between espresso shots. *(Sakura +1)*

---

### Path B — Browse Ink & Pages (the bookshop)

The door creaks open to reveal towering shelves overflowing with books. A cat sleeps on a stack of paperbacks. **Akira** looks up from a crossword — he's the quiet owner with reading glasses. He doesn't get many visitors.

**Choice:**
- *"Any recommendations for a newcomer?"* → Akira +2. He hands you *Letters from a Small Town* — a novel about someone discovering magic in a new place.
- *"This place is amazing — how many books do you have?"* → Akira +1. He says he stopped counting at four thousand.
- *"Just looking around."* → No points. He tells you the cat's name is Hemingway.

If you engage, he warms up slightly. *(Akira +1)*

---

### Path C — Just head to your apartment

You settle into a small studio above a bakery. From the window you see the main street — the café and bookshop beckon. You decide to explore tomorrow.

---

### Day 1 Evening (all paths converge)

You unpack by the window as cherry-blossom petals drift past the glass. "Maybe this year won't be so bad after all." You drift off to sleep.

---

## Chapter 2 — Settling In

Day two. Saturday morning, the street is alive with shopkeepers. You have the whole day free.

### Choice: How do you spend your Saturday?

*(Options depend on what you visited in Chapter 1)*

---

### Path: Visit Petal Brew *(only if you haven't been yet)*

Sakura is delighted. She insists on a lavender latte. *(Sakura +1)* She mentions the **Spring Festival** — she's doing a live-painting booth and invites you to come. *(Sakura +1)*

---

### Path: Visit Ink & Pages *(only if you haven't been yet)*

Akira is surprised you came. "Not many people seek out bookshops twice." *(Akira +1)* He mentions the Spring Festival — he's running a poetry reading corner. *(Akira +1)*

---

### Path: Go to the park

You find a peaceful park at the edge of town, surrounded by cherry trees. A pond reflects the pink canopy. Your mind goes quiet for the first time in months.

You notice a flyer: **"Hanamachi Spring Festival — Volunteers Needed!"**

**Choice:**
- *Take the flyer and sign up* → Festival helped flag set.
- *Leave it* → You enjoy the silence.

---

### Path: Help set up the Spring Festival booth

You help the committee build stalls. Sakura and Akira both show up. The three of you spend the afternoon building booths, stringing lights, and arguing about banner colours. *(Sakura +1, Akira +1)*

---

### Chapter 2 Evening (all paths converge)

Your phone buzzes with a text message:

- **If Sakura points ≥ Akira points AND you visited the café:** Sakura texts: *"Hey! Thanks for today. See you at the festival? 🌸"*
  - *"Wouldn't miss it!"* → Sakura +1
  - *"Maybe, we'll see."* → No change
- **If Akira points > Sakura points AND you visited the shop:** Akira texts: *"I set aside a book I think you'd like. Come by whenever."*
  - *"I'll be there tomorrow."* → Akira +1
  - *"Thanks, I'll try."* → No change
- **Otherwise:** No messages. The town still feels new.

You fall asleep to the sound of wind chimes.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Spring Festival

Hanamachi is transformed — paper lanterns, food stalls, crafts, and games. The whole town is here.

### Choice: Where do you go?

---

### Path: Sakura's live-painting booth

Sakura is painting the festival in real time with children watching. She asks you to hold her palette.

**Choice:**
- *"I'd love to!"* → Sakura +3. You hold her palette while she paints. She says: *"Most people think art is about talent. But it's really about paying attention. You seem like someone who pays attention."*
- *"I'll watch from here."* → Sakura +1. You watch her paint from a distance.

---

### Path: Akira's poetry corner

Akira is in a quiet alcove lit by string lights. He's about to read one of his own poems.

**Choice:**
- *"I'd like to hear it."* → Akira +3. He reads: *"We are letters waiting to be read, / pages pressed between the days, / ink still wet from words unsaid — / quiet in our quiet ways."* You tell him it's beautiful. He opens up, vulnerable.
- *"Sure, go ahead."* → Akira +1. He reads softly.

---

### Path: Wander on your own

You enjoy the stalls — mochi, goldfish scooping, a folk band. If you have points with Sakura, she waves from across the square *(Sakura +1)*. If you have points with Akira, he gives you a brief nod *(Akira +1)*.

---

### Festival Evening — The Lanterns

Night falls. Paper lanterns are released from the river bank. Hanamachi glows gold.

**Who you watch the lanterns with depends on your points:**

---

#### Lanterns with Sakura *(Sakura ≥ 5 and Sakura > Akira)*

You stand side by side at the riverbank. Sakura says if you make a wish on a lantern, it'll come true. You ask what she'd wish for. She says: *"I think my wish already came true. Someone new to share this with."* *(Sakura +2)*

---

#### Lanterns with Akira *(Akira ≥ 5 and Akira > Sakura)*

Akira leads you to a stone bridge where lanterns float beneath you, reflections doubling the light. He says he comes here every year and it's always been solitary. You say: "Not this year." He agrees. *(Akira +2)*

---

#### Lanterns with Both *(Sakura ≥ 3 and Akira ≥ 3)*

All three of you watch the sky fill with light. Sakura declares it the best festival ever. Akira admits it's "up there."

---

#### Lanterns Alone *(low points)*

You watch the lanterns from your apartment window. They're beautiful, but you feel like you're watching from the outside. You think: *"Maybe I should put myself out there more…"*

---

## Chapter 4 — Crossroads

Weeks pass. Spring deepens into early summer. You settle into a rhythm of classes, study sessions, and visits to the café and bookshop.

**Your route is determined by accumulated points:**

| Condition | Route |
|-----------|-------|
| Sakura ≥ 6 and Sakura > Akira | **Sakura Route** |
| Akira ≥ 6 and Akira > Sakura | **Akira Route** |
| Sakura ≥ 4 and Akira ≥ 4 | **Friendship Route** |
| Otherwise | **Solo Route** |

---

## Sakura Route

You've been spending evenings at Petal Brew after closing, watching Sakura paint. She tells you she got accepted into an art program in the city — a summer intensive. It's an incredible opportunity, but she'd have to leave for three months.

### Final Choice:

---

### "You should go. This is your dream." / "I'd miss you, but I think you should do it."

She's moved — you're the first person to say that without a "but" attached. She asks if you'll watch the lanterns with her when she comes back. You say: "It's a date."

> **ENDING: Colours of Tomorrow** 🌅
>
> Sakura leaves for the city. You exchange real paper letters all summer, each one decorated with her sketches. When autumn comes, she's the first one off the bus. She's carrying a new canvas — a painting of you, sitting in Petal Brew, illuminated by lantern light.
>
> *"Some distances only make the heart more vivid."*

---

### "Do you really want to leave everything here?"

She hesitates. The pull of ambition clashes with comfort. She stays. The summer is lovely — painting together, lazy mornings at the café. But sometimes she looks at the horizon with a question she never asks.

> **ENDING: Still Waters** 🌊
>
> Life in Hanamachi is gentle. Sakura keeps painting. The café never changes. It's peaceful. It's safe. But on quiet nights, you wonder what colours she might have found in the city.
>
> *"Some doors, once closed, stay closed."*

---

## Akira Route

You've been spending afternoons at Ink & Pages. Akira saves a chair for you by the window. One day, he pulls out a handwritten manuscript — hundreds of pages. A novel he's been writing for three years. No one has read it. He wants your honest opinion before deciding whether to send it to a publisher.

### Final Choice:

---

### "I'd be honoured. I'll read every page." / "Only if you're sure. This is very personal."

You stay up all night reading. The novel is about a small-town shopkeeper who befriends a stranger and learns to open up. It's beautiful — quiet, honest, deeply human. You tell Akira it's extraordinary. For the first time, he truly smiles.

> **ENDING: Between the Lines** 📖
>
> Akira sends the manuscript. Months later, a small publisher picks it up. The dedication reads: *"For [your name] — who read me before anyone else did."* On the day the first copy arrives, you sit together in the shop with Hemingway purring between you.
>
> *"Some stories only need one reader to become real."*

---

### "Maybe you should ask someone more qualified."

He slides the manuscript back into the drawer. The lock clicks. "Forget I mentioned it." The distance returns to his eyes. You keep visiting, but a wall has been rebuilt brick by brick.

> **ENDING: Unfinished Pages** 📕
>
> Akira never sends the manuscript. Ink & Pages stays the same — quiet, dusty, beautiful. You graduate and leave wondering what his novel was really about. Years later, in a secondhand shop in another city, you find a slim book with a familiar name on the spine. You buy it. You never open it.
>
> *"Some stories are easier left unread."*

---

## Friendship Route

Spring turns to summer. The three of you are inseparable — coffee at Petal Brew, reading at Ink & Pages, walks under the cherry trees. Sakura and Akira want to create an art-and-poetry exhibit for the Summer Festival. They ask for your help.

### Choice:

---

### "Count me in!"

You spend weeks preparing. Sakura paints, Akira writes, you bring it all together. On festival night, the exhibit draws the biggest crowd Hanamachi has ever seen.

> **ENDING: Three Colours** 🎨
>
> The exhibit becomes an annual tradition. Sakura's paintings hang beside Akira's poems, and your name is on every programme as co-curator. Hanamachi stays small. But with friends like these, it never feels that way.
>
> *"Love isn't always romantic. Sometimes it's two people who make a small town feel like the whole world."*

---

### "I'll support you both from the crowd."

You watch from the crowd as they unveil their exhibit. It's wonderful. They wave at you from across the square.

> **ENDING: Gentle Distance** 🌙
>
> You stay close to both of them, but always a step back. It's comfortable. It's safe. When you leave Hanamachi, you carry two friendships that shaped you in quiet, lasting ways.

---

## Solo Route

The weeks pass quietly. You study, attend class, keep mostly to yourself. Hanamachi is peaceful, but you haven't found your place in it. One warm evening, you watch the sunset paint the rooftops gold and think: "There's still time."

> **ENDING: Open Road** 🛤️
>
> You finish the year and leave Hanamachi with good grades and a quiet appreciation for small-town life. No grand romance, no dramatic goodbye — just the memory of cherry blossoms and the knowledge that some chapters are bridges to the next.
>
> *"The road ahead is wide open."*
